# Scoring rubric (owned by the Judge)

Score each criterion 1-5. Weighted total = sum(score / 5 x weight), out of 100. Weights favour
real demand, speed to first cash and downside containment over the size of the eventual outcome.
Edit the weights to match your goal; keep them summing to 100.

| # | Criterion | Weight | 1 means | 5 means |
|---|-----------|--------|---------|---------|
| 1 | Evidence of demand | 20 | No proof anyone would pay | Paying customers or strong signed intent already seen |
| 2 | Time to first dollar | 15 | 12+ months | Under 30 days |
| 3 | Distribution | 10 | No identified way to reach buyers cheaply | A proven channel (search, marketplace, community, existing audience) a newcomer can win |
| 4 | Founder fit and learnability | 10 | Needs credentials, licences or a network that cannot be acquired in time | Skills held or learnable in weeks |
| 5 | Margin and unit economics | 10 | Thin or negative, high CAC | 70%+ gross margin, payback under 3 months |
| 6 | Scalability and sellability | 10 | Revenue capped by the founder's hours, nothing a buyer would want | Decouples from hours; recurring, documented, transferable |
| 7 | Solo start and capital | 10 | Needs a team or large cash | One person, near-zero cash |
| 8 | Defensibility | 5 | Trivially copied | Compounding moat (data, network, brand, switching cost) |
| 9 | Downside containment | 5 | Can lose savings or face legal exposure | Cheap to stop, little to lose |
| 10 | Founder motivation | 5 | Would not enjoy it | Would want to work on it for a year |

## Verdict bands (a guide; the Judge may override with a stated reason)
| Total | Default ruling |
|-------|----------------|
| 75-100 | PURSUE |
| 55-74 | TEST (run the pre-registered experiment first) |
| 40-54 | PARK (name the reopen condition) |
| below 40 | KILL |

## Override rules
- Demand scored 1 or 2 caps the ruling at TEST, regardless of total.
- Downside scored 1 caps the ruling at PARK unless the risk can be removed.
- Distribution scored 1 caps the ruling at PARK.
- A legal or regulatory blocker the founder cannot clear caps the ruling at PARK.
- A strong argument from one seat never moves the total; only evidence does.

## Goal gate
Every ruling states the probability band (low under 15%, medium 15-40%, high over 40%) of reaching
the founder's target income by their deadline (both in `founder-profile.md`), with the
month-by-month milestones that would have to occur. PURSUE needs at least medium. An idea that
cannot plausibly hit the target in time may still be PARKED as a longer play, but the ruling must
say so plainly.

If the founder plans to sell the business, also state the probability band of having a sale-ready
asset (documented trailing profit, recurring revenue, low owner dependence) by the planned date,
and a realistic sale value range.

## Note
This is a filter for ordering ideas, not a prediction. A 90 on bad data is worse than a 60 on good
data. Record confidence (low / medium / high) next to every total.
